## ⚡ Streamlined Features & Updates

### 1. Brand Theme: Blue & White (as per Logo)
- High-contrast, clean corporate aesthetic using the exact royal blue and deep navy colors extracted from your high-res `R.png` logo.
- Clear readability, crisp typography, and uncluttered layout.

### 2. Client Details: 100% Optional
- **Client GST, PAN, Phone, Email, and Address are completely optional.**
- If Client GSTIN or PAN is left blank, the invoice automatically omits those rows without any awkward placeholders. Only the details you actually enter will be displayed.
- Flexible **Tax Treatment** selector:
  - `Intra-State: CGST (9%) + SGST (9%)`
  - `Inter-State: IGST (18%)`
  - `No Tax / 0% GST` (for exempt/non-GST billing)
  - `Auto-Detect by Client State`

### 3. Clean Services & Pricing Table (No SAC)
- SAC / HSN codes have been removed for a clean, straightforward billing format.
- Preloaded with Radhvena's 9 core technology and digital growth service presets from `radhvena.tech` for instant 1-click line item entry.
- Enter Service Title, scope description, quantity, unit price, item discount, and GST rate.

### 4. Simple Terms & Computer-Generated Notice (No Signature Needed)
- Simplified to clear, friendly statements:
  - *"Payment is due within 15 calendar days from the invoice date."*
  - *"This is a computer-generated invoice and does not require a physical signature."*
  - *"Thank you for choosing Radhvena Technologies!"*
- Clean footer without stamp or signing lines.

### 5. Removed Bank Details & QR
- Bank remittance and UPI QR sections have been removed from both the form and invoice sheet for a clean, spacious look.

### 6. Date Controls & Quick Presets
- Edit Invoice Date and Due Date anytime.
- Quick Due Date presets: `Due on Receipt`, `+7 Days`, `+15 Days`, `+30 Days`.
- One-click `Auto Next No` to increment invoice numbers sequentially.

### 7. Automated Indian Number-to-Words & A4 PDF Print
- Automatic Indian numbering translation (e.g., *"INR One Lakh Eighteen Thousand Only"*).
- Click **"🖨️ Download PDF / Print"** for vector-sharp standard A4 PDF printing.
